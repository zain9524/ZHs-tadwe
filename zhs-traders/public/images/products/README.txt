PRODUCT IMAGES
==============

Place your product images in this folder.

Naming convention:
  nutraceutical-materials.jpg
  vitamin-compounds.jpg
  industrial-seals.jpg
  packaging-materials.jpg
  general-materials.jpg

After adding an image, update the "image" field in:
  src/data/products.js

Example:
  image: "/images/products/your-image-name.jpg"

Recommended image size: 800 x 600 px (4:3 ratio), JPG or WebP.
